zbCAD

A small set of drafting, layout, scheduling and takeoff tools for construction work, plus guides on the fundamentals. Each tool runs in the browser: no install, no account, just open it and start working.

Tools

  • zbCAD

    2D drafting with a typed command line, object snaps, layers and associative dimensions. Opens and saves DXF and plots to scaled vector PDF.

    Open/Documentation
  • Sheet Metal Layout

    Flat patterns for rectangle-to-round transitions, cones, offset reducers, elbows and branches. Exports DXF and SVG, or prints full size across multiple sheets.

  • Taper Studio

    Tapered roof insulation layout: drains, crickets and saddles, panel layout and takeoff, and average R-value. Exports a plan along with DXF, Excel and CSV.

  • CPM Scheduler

    Critical path scheduling with a Gantt chart: finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish and start-to-finish links with lag, work calendars and holidays, float, baselines and progress. Imports and exports MS Project XML.

  • Wind Zone Visualizer

    Roof and wall wind zone geometry per ASCE 7-22, drawn to the building's actual dimensions.

Guides

  • Estimating from First Principles

    How to build a construction estimate from the ground up: scope, logistics, quantity takeoff, materials, labor by crew and production rate, and general conditions, with what usually gets missed at each step.

These tools are built for a desktop with a mouse and keyboard, and are works in progress. Check anything that matters against the governing code and manufacturer data.
