zbCAD

Drafting, layout, scheduling and takeoff tools for construction work, plus guides on the fundamentals. Everything runs in the browser: no install, no account.

Your work stays on your computer. The drawings, photos and files you open or make here are never uploaded. Each tool does its work right in your browser and saves to your own device. The site keeps nothing of yours; it only counts visits.

Drafting

  • zbCAD

    2D drafting with a typed command line, snaps, layers and associative dimensions. DXF in and out, plots to scaled PDF.
    Documentation
  • Sheet Metal Layout

    Flat patterns for transitions, cones, reducers, elbows and branches, printed full size or exported to DXF.

Roofing

  • Wind Zone Visualizer

    FM Global 1-28 roof and wall wind zones, drawn to the building's real dimensions in 3D and plan.
  • Taper Studio

    Tapered insulation layout with crickets and saddles, takeoff and average R-value.
  • Metal Panel Layout

    Numbered panel layouts and rollformer-ready cut lists from field dimensions.

Field

  • Roof Overlay

    Lay a floor plan over an aerial photo to find a leak or a room on the roof.
  • 360 Photo Viewer

    Look around a 360° photo and save ordinary photos of any part of it.

Scheduling

  • CPM Scheduler

    Critical path scheduling on a Gantt chart, with MS Project XML in and out.
  • Deadline PlannerPhone

    Plan a day's work backwards from a deadline, with alerts before each step.

Guides

  • Estimating from First Principles

    Building an estimate from scope and takeoff through labor and general conditions.

Built for a desktop with a mouse and keyboard unless marked otherwise. All of it is a work in progress: check anything that matters against the governing code and manufacturer data.
